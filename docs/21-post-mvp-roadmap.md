# Post-MVP Roadmap

Roadmap items are hypotheses, not commitments. They do not enter MVP architecture beyond stable categories, versioned templates, proof types, and transactional rewards already justified today.

## V1 — Prove and harden the loop

- Expand curated catalog and launch areas based on no-match data.
- Manual area selection and basic unavailable/unsafe reporting if not included at MVP.
- Password recovery, account management polish, shareable private-safe completion card.
- Operational content tooling only to the level catalog maintenance requires.
- Experiment with “Surprise Me / Chaos Mode” as a presentation of Random using the same safe constraints.

Exit signal: reliable supply, safe operations, and repeat completion behavior.

## V1.5 — Context and motivation

- Weather-aware eligibility using a reviewed provider and fallback.
- Simple, non-punitive streaks and a small achievement set.
- Map exploration/history visualization without background tracking.
- Seasonal/city challenges curated by the SideQuest team.
- Better accessibility/location metadata.

## V2 — Social and collaborative exploration

- Friends and privacy controls before any social visibility.
- Party Quests with invitation, synchronization, cancellation, and safety design.
- City challenges and opt-in leaderboards segmented to reduce abuse.
- Creator Quest pilot with moderation, rights, quality, and reporting workflows.
- AI-assisted (not autonomous) drafting of Quest Templates behind human review and deterministic safety gates.

## Long-term vision

- Trusted local exploration network across cities.
- Carefully governed sponsored Quests that remain clearly labeled and constraint-fit.
- Richer verification methods proportional to reward/risk.
- Personalized recommendations with explainability, control, and privacy.
- Ecosystem APIs/creator economy only after moderation, fraud, and unit economics are proven.

## Guardrails

No AI Quest reaches users without moderation; no social feature makes proof/location public by default; no monetization can override budget/safety fit; no map feature implies continuous tracking without a separate explicit privacy case.
