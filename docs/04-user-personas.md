# User Personas

Personas guide tradeoffs; they are not demographic eligibility rules.

## Primary — Maya, the spontaneous urban explorer

- **Context:** 24, early-career professional in Jakarta; often has 30–120 minutes between plans.
- **Behavior:** Uses maps and social media for ideas but abandons searches when choices multiply.
- **Needs:** A fast, affordable suggestion near her current area; confidence that it is open, public, and achievable.
- **Motivation:** Break routine, collect small memories, discover neighborhoods.
- **Frustrations:** Long onboarding, vague recommendations, hidden costs, excessive travel, unsafe suggestions.
- **Success:** Receives and accepts a fitting Quest in under a minute, completes it, and feels motivated by visible progress.

## Secondary — Arif, the low-planning social-adjacent user

- **Context:** 31, remote worker in Bandung; wants a reason to leave home after work, usually alone.
- **Behavior:** Values clear instructions and predictable effort; will not research multiple venues.
- **Needs:** Chill, Active, or Creative Quests that work without a group; recovery when connectivity is unreliable.
- **Motivation:** Improve variety and maintain a sense of momentum.
- **Frustrations:** Childish gamification, forced sharing, inaccessible or closed locations.
- **Success:** Builds a history across categories without feeling pressured into social features.

## Accessibility lens — Sari

- **Context:** Any target user who uses larger text, screen reader support, reduced motion, or has limited walking tolerance.
- **Needs:** Non-color cues, scalable layouts, explicit physical demands, alternatives to motion-heavy celebration, and no assumption that “walking distance” is universally accessible.
- **Product implication:** Quest metadata and instructions must describe physical effort; accessibility needs are not inferred from sensitive health data.

## Deliberately unsupported MVP persona

People seeking coordinated group events, competitive leaderboards, child-focused activities, paid rewards, or expert travel itineraries are not the MVP focus.
